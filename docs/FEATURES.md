# Feature overview

## Regional campaign

The World connects Kanto, Johto, Sevii, Almia, Sinnoh, and Hoenn. Regional
campaign data covers towns, routes, buildings and interiors, caves, tunnels,
Gym progression, League rooms, services, transport, HM access, and scripted
story beats. Generated-region maps use host-provided Kanto/Johto tileset
approximations; native Kanto and Johto tilesets are preserved.

The starting region determines the early level curve. Regions tackled after a
regional completion use the advanced curve rather than placing high-level
trainers into an unfinished starting campaign.

## AI rivals

Rivals maintain persistent teams, inventories, relationships, careers, goals,
memories, regional badges, League clears, and travel state. Their progress is
limited by physical travel, available teams, campaign gates, and the selected
simulation options.

Named rivals include the standard and generated roster plus custom characters
such as DIO, OZZY, MANSON, and ASMONGOLD with their configured signature
partners.

## Factions and world events

Team Rocket, Pokémon Rangers, tournaments, ecology, theft and recovery,
research, world news, and regional incidents share the persistent world state.
Consequences can happen outside the player's current map and later surface
through dialogue, news, encounters, or changed rival behavior.

## Weather and seasons

Embedded Weather FX provides automatic and selected weather, overworld and
battle presentation, typed weather, terrain interaction, time of day, seasons,
audio, rare phenomena, and supported 2D/3D rendering paths. Weather and season
data also influence regional ecology and AI decisions.

## WX Pokémon

WX Pokémon are registered persistent forms associated with environmental
conditions. Recognition covers species IDs, form metadata, starters, tutors,
achievements, owned Pokémon, and WX Pikachu variants.

## Achievements and trainer records

The Start menu includes categorized achievements for each region and activity
type, including exploration, catches, WX Pokémon, weather, fishing, battles,
story events, and holidays. The Trainer Card presents 48 badges and regional
seals across multiple pages.

## Services and convenience

- Regional Poké Marts can provide badge-gated WX starters.
- Pokémon Centers host Wonder Traders.
- WX starter move tutors validate supported evolutionary forms and levels.
- Held-item mode enables hourly regional berry trees.
- Regional Map Cards and World Map access are available across campaigns.
- RivalGear provides contacts, news, world information, and simulation tools.
- Start-menu QOL pages update and persist settings during play.

## Optional mechanics

Players can configure generation scope through Generation 8 and independently
control abilities, held items, expanded moves, modern types, physical/special
split behavior, shiny presentation, wild encounter ownership, AI simulation,
weather, WX generation, achievements, and display features.
